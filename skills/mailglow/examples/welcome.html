A note from the founder

Your workspace is ready, Maya.

Fieldnote is where your team’s interviews, observations and loose ideas stop getting lost. It works best when it is a little boring: one place, used every day.

Here is what I would do in your first week. None of it takes more than ten minutes.
1

Write one note, badly

Paste the last thing a customer told you. Rough is fine — you can tidy it later, and the habit matters more than the prose.
2

Invite the person who takes the most calls

Usually support or sales. They hear the most and write the least; Fieldnote is built to make that easy for them.
3

Tag three notes with the same word

That is a theme. Themes are the whole point: they turn a pile of quotes into something you can bring to a planning meeting.
Write your first note →

What you can ignore for now

Integrations, templates and permissions. They are useful in month two. In week one they are a way to feel busy without writing anything down.

If anything is confusing, reply to this email. It comes to me, and I read every one.
