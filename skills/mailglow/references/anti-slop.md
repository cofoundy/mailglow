# Email anti-slop — the tells that make an email look generated

Measured on a real round (3 directions × 3 emails scored 18/32 in an Impeccable critique; the founder's
verdict: "ok-ish, mucho AI slop"). Every item below appeared in that round. Delete on sight, then check the
email still says what it must.

## Structure
- **The scaffold**: header → eyebrow → headline → quote → button → labelled log → key/value rows → footer.
  If your three "directions" share it and only the colour changes, you have one direction in three paints.
- **Card in a card in a card**: a tinted box inside a card holding bubbles. One surface.
- **Side-stripe**: a 3–4 px coloured left border on a tinted box to mean "important". Banned.
- **Identical rows**: a digest of N same-weight cards + a "Total" that repeats the headline. Rank instead:
  the first item carries the weight and the only button, the rest are one line.
- **Key/value tables** (`Canal: WhatsApp`, `Te invita: …`) — the admin-panel reflex. Say it once in a sentence
  or a single meta line.

## Type & labels
- **Eyebrows**: uppercase, letter-spaced labels above everything ("QUÉ NECESITA · NOTA DE LA IA"). Zero.
- **"·" chains** of 3–4 facts. One meta line at most, once.
- **Serif "for elegance"** with no reason the product would speak that way.
- **Pull-quote glyph** (a giant “) + byline to dress up a note.
- **Mono timestamps** as a "technical" costume.

## Decoration
- **"→" inside every button and link.**
- **Emoji as icons** (📎 pill, 🔥 subject). Show the real thing (the attachment thumbnail) or plain words.
- **Pills/badges** for facts that are just words.

## Copy
- **System voice** in the headline ("Requiere humano", "Nota de la IA", "Escalo…"). The reader thinks in the
  customer's words: "Ana dice que ya pagó S/120".
- **The same slogan cadence in every email** ("X te espera." with a punchy period).
- **Headline = event** ("someone waits") instead of **headline = the ask/outcome** ("Revisa el pago de Ana").

## What authored looks like instead
- The headline is the reader's next action or the customer's ask, in their words.
- The layout borrows from an artefact in the reader's world (a kitchen ticket, the WhatsApp chat itself, a
  bank alert) — structurally, not as a texture.
- One gesture owns the email (e.g. a full-bleed band holding the fact and the button). Everything else is quiet.
- Different urgency tiers change structure, not only colour.
